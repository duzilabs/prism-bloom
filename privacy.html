Prism Bloom – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Developer: Duzi Labs

This policy explains what information the mobile game Prism Bloom (“the game”, “we”) uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: we don’t run our own servers, we don’t ask you to create an account, and we don’t sell your data. The game uses Google services for ads, purchases and leaderboards, and those services process some data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress is kept only on your phone: best scores, gems, unlocked themes, goals, settings (language, sound, vibration and so on) and the game in progress. We never receive this information.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this progress in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, an occasional ad between games, and optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (city or region, not your exact location);
  • information about your device (model, operating system, language) and how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostic information about how ads perform.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads on some phones).

Buying Remove Ads turns off the banner and the between-game ads. Optional reward ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.

Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases (gem packs and Remove Ads) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your name, address or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of what was bought so it can give you the item. Google’s handling of payments is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you use Google Play Games, the game sends your best scores to Google Play Games leaderboards. Your Play Games profile name, avatar and scores may then be visible to other players, depending on your Play Games profile settings. Google Play Games is operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings.

5. What we don’t do

  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own, and no data sold to anyone.
  • No account registration, and no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • The game asks for these Android permissions only: internet and network state (for ads, purchases and leaderboards) and vibration (for haptic feedback). The ads library also uses the advertising ID permission.

6. Children

Prism Bloom is a puzzle game suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game in a way that shared personal information, contact us and we will help.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Game progress: delete it any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the game.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google according to its own retention policies. Reset or delete your advertising ID to break the link to past ad activity.
  • Leaderboard scores: managed through your Google Play Games profile.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to some uses of it. Because we don’t hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled through the Google controls described above. If you have questions or a request, contact us and we will help you.

9. Security

Connections made by the game to Google services are encrypted (HTTPS). Progress stored on your phone is protected by Android’s app sandbox.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game’s update notes.
